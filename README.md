# One Word for the Boss — "mindreader"

Entry word: **mindreader**

## Why this angle

The bounty asks for exactly one word describing your boss, and the judge rewards words that are funny on their own, true, and fresh — not the first word anyone would reach for. "mindreader" is the boss who never tells you what they actually want, then acts betrayed that you didn't already know. No brief, no spec, no hint — just a sigh and "you should have known." Everyone who has ever worked for anyone has met this boss: the one who treats telepathy as a job requirement and silence as a management strategy.

It lands without a caption, because the joke is the word itself. It is true in a way that stings a little — the unspoken expectation, the test you didn't know you were taking. It is fresh: the field will be crowded with "micromanager", "tyrant" and "dictator", the words everyone reaches for first, while "mindreader" comes at the same boss from the side nobody else is standing on. And it travels — a kitchen, a warehouse or an office all have a boss who expects you to read their mind.

## How to run

Requires Node 22 or later. No dependencies, no network, no randomness.

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry word to `/output/entry.txt` and self-checks the gate first: 1–30 characters, exactly one word, not only whitespace. (To write elsewhere locally, set `OUTPUT_DIR`.)
